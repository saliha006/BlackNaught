How it works

One fault. Different answers.

The same power cut means a phone check for one household and a 999 escalation for the next. Blacknaught works out which is which.

Predicts how long the power stays off

A survival model trained on every unplanned outage Electricity North West has published, conditioned on how long a cut has already lasted.

Private by design

No home is ever drawn. Sensitive fields are AES-256-GCM encrypted, and the map only shows cells with 11 or more people.

An agent that checks its own work

Every prediction is graded once its horizon passes. If accuracy drifts, the agent recalibrates and logs why.

Two houses, one fault

Caution scales with how critical the patient is, not with how bad the area usually is. That keeps alerts rare enough to be trusted.
