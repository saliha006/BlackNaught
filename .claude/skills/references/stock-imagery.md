# Stock & open imagery: licence terms checked word for word (2026-10-16)

**Headline correction: Stills (stills.com) is NOT free and NOT "no-copyright".** It is a *paid*, curated licensing service from FM LLC (sister brands Musicbed and Filmsupply). Use it only if the client pays for a licence.

"Free" falls into three different tiers. Don't mix them up:
- **CC0 / public domain:** no copyright restrictions. Third-party rights (people, trademarks) can still apply.
- **Free custom licence** (Unsplash, Pexels): free commercial use, but with listed prohibitions. Not CC0.
- **Paid licence** (Stills): fee, attribution, per-project scope.

Practical rules for the agent:
- Prefer **CC0 museum sources** when provenance matters. Prefer Unsplash/Pexels for modern lifestyle shots.
- Store the source URL and licence line next to every image you add (a comment or a credits file).
- Never use people's faces in "endorsement" positions (testimonials, "our customers") from any free source.
- Never use any stock image as part of a logo or trademark.
- AI-training use is not addressed by most licences. Ask before scraping a set.
